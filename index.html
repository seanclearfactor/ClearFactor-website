About Us

A factor built around the owner, not the other way round.

ClearFactor was founded to fix what owners dislike most about factoring: vague invoices, slow replies, and no idea what's actually being spent. We combine local Scottish property expertise with AI-assisted operations to stay responsive at a scale most factors can't match.

Customer Support

Answers, before you have to ask twice.

The most common reasons owners get in touch, answered up front.

Debt Recovery

If an owner's account falls into arrears, we follow a fixed process: a friendly reminder, a formal notice, then — only if needed — referral for recovery. Interest and reasonable recovery costs may be added at that final stage.

Struggling to pay? Contact us before a payment is missed. We can usually agree a short-term plan rather than let an account fall behind.

Homeowner Guides

Plain-English guides cover the topics owners ask about most: what factoring actually covers, how the float works, how repairs get approved, and what happens when you sell your flat.

Guides are published to My ClearFactor as they're written — request a specific one from your property manager if it isn't there yet.

Emergency Contacts

For anything that puts the building or a resident at immediate risk — burst pipes, storm damage, a common door that won't secure — call the 24-hour line. It's triaged instantly and, where needed, a contractor is instructed the same day.

Not urgent? Log it through My ClearFactor instead and it's handled in the normal repairs queue.

Insurance Claims

Report damage to common property as soon as it's found. We log the claim with the block insurer, arrange any necessary inspection, and keep owners updated until it's settled.

Claims on an individual flat's own contents are between the owner and their personal insurer — we can point you to the block policy documents if needed.

Make a Payment

Pay your account, your way.

Every method reaches the same client account, ring-fenced from ClearFactor's own funds.

Direct debit

Set once, taken automatically each period. The simplest way to stay ahead of arrears.

Bank transfer

Pay by BACS or online banking using your account reference — found on every invoice.

Card, online

Pay a single invoice or top up your float by debit or credit card through My ClearFactor.

Payment plans

If a bill catches you out, get in touch before it's due — short-term plans are agreed case by case.

Ways to pay

Direct debit is our recommended method — set it up once through My ClearFactor and never miss a due date. One-off payments can be made by card online, or by bank transfer using the reference on your invoice.

Payment rules

Invoices are issued in line with the schedule in your written statement of service, and are due within the period stated on the invoice. The float is topped up when it falls below the level agreed for your building, so routine costs can still be met without delay.

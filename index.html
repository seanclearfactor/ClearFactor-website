Make a Payment

Pay your account, your way.

Simple, secure ways to pay your factoring account.

Bank transfer

Pay by BACS or online banking, using the bank details and account reference shown on your invoice.

Debit or credit card

Pay securely online by debit or credit card using the payment link on your invoice.

Payment plans

If a bill catches you out, get in touch before it's due. Short-term plans are agreed case by case.

Ways to pay

You can pay by bank transfer using the bank details and reference on your invoice, or by debit or credit card using the secure payment link on your invoice. If you pay by bank transfer, always quote your account reference so your payment is matched to your account straight away.

Invoices and your balance

Invoices and statements are sent to you by email, so you always have a copy to hand.

Not sure what you owe, or whether a payment has reached us? Just ask. Email enquiries@clearfactor.co.uk at any time and we'll send you an up-to-date balance.

Protecting yourself from fraud

Only use the bank details printed on your ClearFactor invoice. We will never email, text or phone you to tell you our bank details have changed. If you receive a message like this, don't make the payment. Email enquiries@clearfactor.co.uk to check with us first.

Payment rules

How often you're invoiced is set out in your Written Statement of Services (WSS). Depending on your building, this can be monthly or quarterly. Each invoice is due within the period stated on it.

The float is topped up when it falls below the level agreed for your building, so routine costs can still be met without delay.

FAQs

Can't find what you need? Email enquiries@clearfactor.co.uk and we'll help, or send you a plain-English guide on the topic.

Paying your account

How often will I be invoiced?

It's set out in your Written Statement of Services (WSS): monthly or quarterly, depending on your building. Need a copy? Just ask.

I've paid, but it isn't showing on my invoice.

Payments made after an invoice is produced appear on the next one, and bank transfers can take a few working days.

If you paid before the invoice date and it's still missing, email enquiries@clearfactor.co.uk with the date, amount and reference.

What if I can't pay on time?

Get in touch before the payment is due. We can usually agree a short-term plan.

What happens if an account isn't paid?

We send a reminder, then a formal notice. Only if it's still unpaid is the account referred for recovery, and interest and reasonable recovery costs may then be added.

The float and selling

What is the float?

A one-off sum each owner pays, held for the building so routine costs can be paid straight away. It's usually due with your first invoice and topped up if it runs low. It still belongs to you.

I'm selling my property. What do I need to do?

Tell us your sale date and your solicitor's details. Until we know about the sale, the account stays in your name.

We'll prepare a final account up to your date of entry and return your float once it's settled.

Repairs and insurance

How do I report damage to the common parts?

Email enquiries@clearfactor.co.uk as soon as you spot it. If it's an insurance matter, we log the claim with the block insurer, arrange any inspection and keep you updated until it's settled.

What about damage inside my own flat?

Claims for your own flat's contents go through your personal insurer. If you need the block policy documents, we can send them.

Complaints

How do I make a complaint?

Email enquiries@clearfactor.co.uk and tell us what's gone wrong. Our complaints procedure, including how long each stage takes, is set out in your Written Statement of Services, and we can send you a copy at any time.

If you're still unhappy once our procedure is complete, or we haven't replied within the timescale it sets out, you can apply to the First-tier Tribunal for Scotland (Housing and Property Chamber).

This policy covers our website and enquiries. If you're an owner in a building we factor, your Written Statement of Services and the privacy notice we give you as a customer explain how we use your information to manage your account.

What we collect

  • What you tell us in our enquiry form: your name, email address, phone number (if you give it), the property or development address, and the details of your enquiry.
  • What you send us by email.

Our website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking, and doesn't load anything from advertising or social media companies.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and, if you ask, prepare a quote. We rely on this being a step you've asked us to take before entering into a contract, and on our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us.

We won't send you marketing, and we never sell your information.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers who handle information only on our instructions: Microsoft, which provides our email and the service that delivers website enquiries to our inbox, and GitHub, which hosts this website and, like most web hosts, keeps short-term technical logs such as IP addresses for security. If your enquiry is about a repair or insurance claim, we may share the relevant details with the contractor or insurer dealing with it.

Some of these providers may process information outside the UK. Where they do, it's protected by safeguards approved under UK data protection law.

How long we keep it

If your enquiry doesn't lead to us working together, we delete it within 12 months. If you become a customer, we keep your information for as long as we manage your building and afterwards for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or object to us using it. Email enquiries@clearfactor.co.uk and we'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.
